Exercise Guide

Bench Press

Builds your chest, shoulders and triceps.

View Exercise

How To Do It

  • Lie flat on a bench with your feet on the floor.
  • Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder width.
  • Lower the bar slowly to your chest.
  • Push the bar back up until your arms are straight.

Muscles Worked

Chest, shoulders and triceps.

Squats

Builds strength in your legs and glutes.

View Exercise

How To Do It

  • Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart.
  • Keep your chest up and back straight.
  • Bend your knees and lower your body down.
  • Push through your heels to stand back up.

Muscles Worked

Quads, hamstrings, glutes and core.

Pull Ups

Improves back and arm strength.

View Exercise

How To Do It

  • Hold the pull-up bar with your palms facing away.
  • Keep your body straight and core tight.
  • Pull yourself up until your chin is above the bar.
  • Lower yourself back down slowly.

Muscles Worked

Back, biceps and shoulders.

Deadlift

A powerful exercise for total body strength.

View Exercise

How To Do It

  • Stand with your feet hip-width apart.
  • Grip the bar with your hands just outside your legs.
  • Keep your back straight and chest lifted.
  • Push through your heels and lift the bar up.

Muscles Worked

Back, glutes, hamstrings, core and traps.

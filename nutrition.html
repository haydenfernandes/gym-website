Nutrition & Healthy Recipes

High Protein Chicken Bowl

A balanced meal packed with protein for muscle growth and recovery.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 200g grilled chicken breast
  • 1 cup brown rice
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Instructions

Cook the rice and vegetables. Grill the chicken until fully cooked. Combine everything in a bowl and drizzle with olive oil.

Benefits

High in protein and carbohydrates to support muscle recovery.

Nutrition Information

  • Calories: 550 kcal
  • Protein: 45g
  • Carbohydrates: 50g
  • Fat: 15g
  • Fibre: 6g

Protein Oats

A healthy breakfast that gives long-lasting energy.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup oats
  • 1 scoop protein powder
  • 1 banana
  • 250ml milk

Instructions

Cook the oats with milk. Stir in protein powder and top with banana slices.

Benefits

Great for breakfast because it provides protein and slow-release energy.

Nutrition Information

  • Calories: 420 kcal
  • Protein: 30g
  • Carbohydrates: 45g
  • Fat: 10g
  • Fibre: 7g

Protein Smoothie

A quick post-workout drink for recovery.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 banana
  • 1 scoop whey protein
  • 250ml milk
  • 1 tablespoon peanut butter

Instructions

Add all ingredients to a blender. Blend until smooth and serve chilled.

Benefits

Useful after training because it helps with energy and recovery.

Nutrition Information

  • Calories: 350 kcal
  • Protein: 32g
  • Carbohydrates: 25g
  • Fat: 12g
  • Fibre: 4g

Salmon & Sweet Potato

A nutritious dinner with protein, healthy fats and carbohydrates.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 salmon fillet
  • 1 sweet potato
  • 1 cup broccoli
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

Bake the salmon and sweet potato. Steam the broccoli and serve together.

Benefits

Contains protein and healthy fats to support recovery and overall health.

Nutrition Information

  • Calories: 600 kcal
  • Protein: 40g
  • Carbohydrates: 45g
  • Fat: 25g
  • Fibre: 8g
